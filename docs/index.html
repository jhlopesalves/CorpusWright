Clean, inspect, and analyse corpora.

The Preprocessing Workflow

A tour of the Prepare workbench

Take a look at how CorpusWright helps you load, clean, and audit your corpus locally.

1. Load and inspect your sources

Load text, HTML, DOCX, or PDF files. The prepare bench shows you raw text side-by-side with cleaning previews, highlighting warnings and structural elements in real time.

2. Configure deterministic cleaning rules

Control exact cleaning rules—like de-hyphenation, unicode normalization, line ending normalization, and noise removal. Every toggled option is saved to your project configuration, making your preprocessing pipeline completely repeatable.

3. Detect and audit repeated artefacts

The Repeated-Artefact Finder automatically scans the entire corpus to identify recurring headers, footers, boilerplate, and extraction noise. Accept or reject candidates to safely build your custom removal rules without risk of silent data loss.

What it is

These commitments shape every part of CorpusWright.

Local-first. Your corpus stays on your machine; nothing is uploaded to a server.

Auditable. Every cleaning step is deterministic and recorded.

Analysis-aware. Preprocessing, concordancing, and analysis stay in one loop.

Statistically grounded. Dispersion (DP), entropy, and frequency distributions such as Zipf and hapax counts are computed transparently.
