Rust corpus workbench

Clean, inspect, and analyse corpora.

A desktop workbench for corpus linguistics, built on a Rust core. The focus is analysis that holds up statistically and methodologically, with modern analytics and metrics as core features rather than add-ons.

Alpha

CorpusWright is under active development. It already handles simple workflows well, but it is not yet a robust, general-purpose statistical platform — that is the direction of travel.

Expect rapid changes to features, documentation, and tutorials.

What it is

Local-first

Your corpus stays on your machine, which matters when texts are private, copyrighted, or institutionally sensitive.

Auditable cleaning

Cleaning is reviewable, deterministic, and explainable by design. Artefacts are always inspectable; whenever the deterministic rules can't catch the noise, you can add custom removals that are reviewable and can be included in the pipeline.

Analysis-aware

Preprocessing is not separate from analysis. KWIC, repeated-artefact review, frequency, dispersion, and collocation belong in the same loop.

What works today

  • Local project folders and source management
  • Text extraction from TXT, HTML, DOCX, and PDF
  • Configurable, deterministic cleaning with processed previews
  • Repeated-artefact finder
  • Structured custom removals
