01 Sources

Read the files before counting

The corpus brings together 10 nineteenth-century works on logic, probability and related subjects. Prepare counts 1,150,196 tokens extracted from plain text, HTML and PDF.

Before preparation, the corpus count includes both the books and their digitisation notices.

02 Repeated artefacts

Review repeated text before removing it

The Repeated Artefact Finder groups recurring lines and short blocks. The highlighted licence fragment occurs 6 times across 3 files.

Review the examples before accepting individual removal rules. Different editions can wrap the same notice differently or carry a different notice.

03 Cleaning

Check which text reaches the counts

Open Flatland in Prepare and compare its Original Text and Processed Text tabs. The original preserves the extracted text, and the processed preview reflects the current preparation settings.

This rule removes 84 tokens across the corpus, leaving 1,150,112. Check what remains in each edition before relying on its counts.

04 Counts

Read frequency with range and dispersion

Frequency counts all occurrences. Range counts the documents in which a type occurs. Gries's DP compares a type's observed shares across documents with the token shares of those documents.

Before cleaning, gutenberg occurred 223 times across 10 works (DP 0.3531). All 223 occurrences remain after applying the reviewed rule, which targets a licence line without that word.

View full-size capture

05 Dispersion

Frequency alone hides how a type is spread

The Dispersion plane plots frequency against DP for the 34,954 observed types. A low DP indicates that a type's occurrences are distributed roughly in proportion to document sizes.

Use the plane to see how different combinations of frequency and dispersion occur in the corpus. Look up a particular word in the Frequency list or read its occurrences in Concordance.

View full-size capture

06 Context

Return from the distribution to the text

Search for probability in Concordance. It appears 1,765 times in 8 works. Distribution shows which books contain the word, and the lines show individual matches in context.

Outside the treatises by Boole, Venn, Laplace and Jevons, 7 of the 17 hits occur in the phrase in all probability. It expresses how likely the writer judges something to be.

07 Record

Keep the record that accompanies the export

The cleaned-corpus export includes preparation settings, warning records and SHA-256 checksums for source and processed files. This export recorded 4 warnings.

Keep the original files and the software release alongside the export. Use the checksums to verify the bytes when you revisit the analysis.
