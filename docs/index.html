What it is

These commitments shape every part of CorpusWright.

Local-first. Your corpus stays on your machine; nothing is uploaded to a server.

Auditable. Every cleaning step is deterministic and recorded, so nothing is silently corrected.

Analysis-aware. Preprocessing, concordancing, and analysis stay in one loop, not scattered across separate tools.

Statistically grounded. Dispersion (DP), entropy, and frequency distributions such as Zipf and hapax counts are treated as core measures, computed transparently.
