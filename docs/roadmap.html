Roadmap

Published alpha, current limits and planned areas

The sections below distinguish capabilities in the latest published alpha from practical limits and broader areas being considered for later work.

Latest published alpha

  • Local project folders and a source inventory
  • Text extraction from supported TXT, HTML, DOCX and PDF sources
  • Configurable cleaning with original and processed previews
  • Repeated-artefact review and structured removal rules
  • Cleaned-text export with configuration and manifest records
  • Corpus overview and surface-frequency views
  • Surface-word and phrase concordances with sorting and source-context preview
  • Document-spread and collocate views, including raw counts, corpus frequency and logDice
  • Strict UTF-8 decoding with Windows-1252 recovery for invalid text input

Known alpha limits

  • The interface and project format may change between alpha releases
  • PDF and OCR results depend on source quality and the selected extraction route
  • Concordance does not yet provide CQL, N-gram browsing, or lemma/POS and annotation-aware queries
  • Analysis export and copy workflows remain limited
  • CorpusWright does not provide remote corpus hosting
  • Unsigned application builds may prompt operating-system warnings

Planned areas

  • N-grams
  • CQL-lite and other structured queries
  • Lemma/POS and annotation-aware search
  • Richer collocation and evidence views
  • Export and copy workflows

These are broad directions rather than a release schedule; their scope may change.

CorpusWright is an open, local-first corpus linguistics workbench, currently in public alpha. View the source on GitHub
